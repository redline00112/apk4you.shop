Help Center

How to Download Games

To access our free downloads, we require users to complete a simple offer. These offers help support our platform and keep it free for everyone.

The offers may include:

  • Trying an app or game
  • Completing a short survey
  • Signing up for a service

Please read each offer before you start and only complete ones you are comfortable with. Thank you for your support!

Download Steps:

  1. Click on the game you want to download
  2. Complete the simple offer (usually takes less than 1 minute)
  3. Get your download link
  4. Install and enjoy!

Installation Tips:

  • Install only from the official store. Don’t enable “Unknown sources”: modified APK files are a common way malware spreads.
  • Some games download extra data the first time you open them, so keep Wi-Fi on and leave enough free storage.
  • If something goes wrong, clear the app’s cache, restart your phone, update the game and your system, or reinstall.
  • Availability, pricing and beta programs are set by the store, so a game may not appear in your country.

Beta programs

Some publishers run official betas. When one is open, you can join it from the game’s store page. Modyx never hosts beta files.
